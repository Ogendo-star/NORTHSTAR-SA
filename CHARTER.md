## 2. Working Agreements & Rules
* **The 4-Hour Rule:** No single task should exceed 4 hours. If a task is too large, it must be broken down into smaller sub-tasks to prevent bottlenecks.
* **Communication:** Daily syncs via WhatsApp standups at 08:00 AM EAT to report blockers and review progress.
* **Commit Standards:** Strict adherence to Conventional Commits format (e.g., `feat:`, `fix:`, `docs:`) to maintain a clean audit log.
